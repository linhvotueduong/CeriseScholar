# Build 1 Phase 9 — Product and accessibility walkthroughs

These walkthroughs define the release behavior to inspect. They are not scripts for evaluating a researcher’s ability or mental state.

## Novice researcher

Start with a broad concern, open Mentor on demand, inspect **What Cerise notices**, choose **Reflect with me**, and read the privacy boundary before asking. Verify that the response says no project change occurred, that an option requires review before addition, and that dismissing it does not alter the pathway.

## Experienced researcher

Open **What I understand**, inspect current route, evidence, unresolved questions, checksums, and artifact health. Change project content after receiving advice. Verify that the old response remains readable but cannot be applied, saved, or reused as a retry.

## Qualitative researcher

Use a qualitative interview or focus-group route. Verify that technique wording supports exploratory/interpretive framing, avoids forcing statistical fields, preserves exact researcher excerpts, and labels generated alternatives as brainstorming unless linked to exact approved evidence.

## Quantitative researcher

Use an online survey or randomized laboratory route. Verify that route and assignment appear as context, that Mentor does not claim power, validity, or readiness, and that the Stage 1 question remains provisional rather than becoming an analysis contract.

## Mixed-methods researcher

Use the mixed-methods fixture and compare multiple source items. Verify that suggestions can distinguish strands and tradeoffs without choosing integration logic or treating one method as authoritative over the other.

## Multilingual researcher

Enter multilingual research text and fill the dynamic table to its 40-row cap. Verify that text remains readable, the provider projection stays under budget, no language is treated as an injection merely because it is non-English, and the UI remains usable at mobile width.

## Keyboard and screen-size checks

- Tab to the Mentor launcher and press Enter.
- Confirm focus moves to the close control.
- Navigate tabs, modes, prompt, request/cancel, and review actions without a pointer.
- Press Escape; confirm the drawer closes and focus returns to the launcher.
- Confirm focused controls are not hidden by the drawer’s scroll boundaries.
- Inspect 1536×1024 and 390×844 layouts without horizontal overflow.
- Confirm motion is nonessential and the support ring stops under reduced motion.
- Confirm availability and failure text is exposed as status/alert content and that offline guidance is clearly labeled as static, local, and non-mutating.
